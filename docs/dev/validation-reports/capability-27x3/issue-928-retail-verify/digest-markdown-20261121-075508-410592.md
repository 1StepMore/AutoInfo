## Executive Summary

Retail developments in this period suggest that brands are likely attempting to extract more value from smaller or more strategically deployed physical footprints, balancing store optimization with the brand-discovery and digital-demand benefits that shopping centers may offer (Source: https://www.retaildive.com/spons/the-shrinking-store-how-retailers-and-restaurants-are-doing-more-with-less/874147/, https://www.retaildive.com/spons/the-mall-advantage-how-shopping-centers-fuel-retail-growth/873797/). ### Key Findings

- **Physical retail footprint optimization**: Retailers and restaurants are reducing store square footage while aiming to maintain or raise revenue targets, requiring brands to optimize operations and space usage to close the gap (Source: https://www.retaildive.com/spons/the-shrinking-store-how-retailers-and-restaurants-are-doing-more-with-less/874147/).
- **Shopping center value**: Physical shopping centers are highlighted as strategically valuable for retail because brick-and-mortar stores can generate brand discovery, stimulate digital demand, and improve operational efficiency beyond point-of-sale transactions (Source: https://www.retaildive.com/spons/the-mall-advantage-how-shopping-centers-fuel-retail-growth/873797/).
- **In-store experience gaps**: Although physical stores still drive most retail sales, unresolved issues such as locked products and confusing self-checkout mean shopper expectations are not being met despite retailer claims that technology or store design can fix these problems (Source: https://www.modernretail.co/podcasts/in-store-shopping-experience-broken/?utm_campaign=modernretaildis&utm_medium=rss&utm_source=general-rss).
- **AI adoption versus consumer trust**: A holiday retail survey found that 63% of retailers use AI in customer communications, yet only 26% of shoppers believe it improves their experience, indicating a disconnect between retailer AI adoption and consumer skepticism (Source: https://www.retaildive.com/spons/holiday-retails-ai-test-from-skeptical-shoppers-to-agents-they-trust-afte/874054/).
- **Inventory automation in practice**: ASICS implemented warehouse drone technology that improved inventory management for its footwear operations, enhancing recovery of misplaced inventory, reducing manual labor hours, and enabling more frequent inventory counts (Source: https://www.retaildive.com/news/asics-boosts-inventory-management-using-warehouse-drones/874481/).
